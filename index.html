Bluffton Park · Old Town Bluffton

Laid-back Lowcountry, all on one quiet street.

Heads Carolina, Tails California is a bright, one-story home in Bluffton Park, a short walk from the shops, galleries, and coffee of Old Town Bluffton.

Start the morning on the front porch while the neighborhood wakes up. Walk the kids to the fishing pond next door to look for turtles, or to the Oscar Frazier Park splash pad on a hot afternoon. Then come home to teal tones, beachy art, a full kitchen, and two big smart TVs for movie night.

It sleeps four with a king room and a twin room, two full bathrooms, laundry, and a backyard with a patio. It's an easy, comfortable base for a family trip, a couple's getaway, a work stay, or a beach week on a budget.

Good to know

Questions guests ask

Is the house dog friendly?

Yes, dogs are welcome. There is a dog park in the neighborhood and a pond next door for walks.

How far is the beach?

Hilton Head Island beaches are roughly 25 to 45 minutes by car, depending on traffic and which beach you pick. Please bring your own beach gear.

Can we walk to Old Town Bluffton?

Yes. Old Town Bluffton, Corner Perk Café, and the Bluffton Promenade are an easy walk from the front door.

How many people does it sleep?

Up to 4 guests: a king bed in one bedroom and two twin beds in the other, with two full bathrooms.

What are check-in and checkout times?

Check-in is after 4:00 PM and checkout is by 10:00 AM. Entry is self check-in.

Where do we park?

Two designated spaces at the rear of the home, plus first-come street parking out front.

Is coffee provided?

There is a Nespresso machine (bring your own pods) and a Keurig with some coffee and condiments to get you started.

Do you offer monthly stays?

Yes, stays of 28 nights or longer are available November through February. Send us your dates.

Why book direct instead of Airbnb?

Booking direct means you deal with Lee and Kristina directly, the owners who manage the home.
